# Play B2.5 — commands, choices and consequences

**A1: modern text-adventure command play. A1a: story and gameplay.** Double-click [Launch B2.5.command](../Launch%20B2.5.command). Installed Godot 4.6.2 is required; this is a silent source candidate, not a standalone app. The illustrated world remains visible above the command history and choices.

Click the input, type an intention and press Return, or click a contextual choice. W/A/S/D and E belong to your text while the input has focus. Click the world or Tab out to use ordinary direct controls. Escape pauses and resumes. Focus loss pauses all action; resume explicitly. **Stop** cancels future work, including an unfinished fetch; a committed step or attack finishes.

Try this opening:

1. `Try blast, then preview shield, then show me dash`. After all three demonstrations, `choose blast` (or shield/dash).
2. `Go to the package, then interact with it`. Entering the actual assessment stops further queued actions so you can make a tactical decision.
3. `Look around`. Use `shoot creature`, `use blast`, `raise shield`, or `dash down`, depending on your power. Move to get range/clear sight. `End turn` deliberately lets the creature respond. Bolt costs 2 AP, blast 2, shield/dash 1; ordinary combat steps cost 1. The same rules govern buttons and text.
4. After victory: `go to package, then collect package`. The pet learns the supply mark.
5. `Go to doorway, then enter shelter`. `Go to desk`, then choose two kits, a field lamp or the cache route. The choices are exclusive.
6. `Go to traveler, then talk to traveler`. `Join traveler`, `decline traveler`, `have companion wait here`, and `rejoin traveler` use the ordinary shelter membership rules.
7. `Go to doorway, then leave shelter`. `Inspect cache` explains two approaches. `Go to cache, then collect cache` requires your travel and preserves its routing label. `Ask the pet to fetch cache` keeps you in place while the pet physically retrieves it, chewing the label. Both yield one kit, once. The method and the traveler's distinct response survive travel and restart.
8. Return to shelter; approach and talk to the traveler. `Save and quit`, then relaunch and `continue` (or use Continue) restores the exact saved game. Old history is readable; it never resumes a command.

`Move 5 up and 8 right` means all five upward steps, then all eight rightward steps. Up decreases the row; right increases the column. It stops at an obstacle, boundary or insufficient AP, without routing around it. `Go to the doorway` instead finds a route to a reachable adjacent interaction point. Completed steps and unexecuted work appear in history.

`Inspect that` uses the recent or clicked target. Ambiguous targets offer named choices (or type their numbered order). `Actually, go to package` replaces future work. `Actually, the other one` uses the preceding target with the last target action; if it has no clear alternative, choose a target. Completed effects cannot be undone.

Questions such as `What would happen if I used shield?` give information without spending AP. `What can I do here?` describes current possibilities. Characters cannot see the outside audience, and shelter is neither owned nor private.

Limitations: this is a bounded English interpreter, not unrestricted conversation. It supports action families and paraphrases, not arbitrary tasks, conditional programs, invented objects or new companion skills. Unknown requests produce guidance without guessing an action. Separate supported requests with `then`; a new place requires fresh target context. No AI service or local model is required. There are no new chapter locations beyond concourse and shelter.

Saves use `dev-state/B2.5-practice-v1`, independent of B2 and historical prototypes. B2 saves are not imported. Save at a stable boundary; failed writes keep the session open. Pause exposes Retry and Restore save access. No owner saves were used in validation. Owner acceptance and full beta readiness remain pending.
