# Product brief

Status: initial synthesis from 48 owner answers; first-experiment plan is a proposal, not a complete production commitment.

Play one human whose ordinary life is interrupted by an alien takeover. Humans are forced into a real space-opera RPG and broadcast to an outside audience. Prepare, explore, use real powers, and make tactical and risk/reward decisions to gain security, access, and ultimately freedom. Ways of thriving emerge through decisions rather than an explicit path selection.

The tone combines dark comedy and satire of entertainment, bureaucracy, and alien society, with some adventure. Earth is not restored. The campaign can end in death, wealth and freedom, or a negotiated alternative. The owner tentatively wants an independent life afterward, including safer replay participation through separate identities that may be saved. A secure organizational job can fund that life. These are long-term directions with unresolved mechanics.

## Initial experience

Create a name and appearance against a shared background. Navigate a neighborhood shopping area during the takeover. Alien orientation involves a welcome package, deliberately released creatures, and temporary shelter. See demonstrations and choose force blast, protective shield, or short dash. Resolve danger in turns; action points are the provisional model. Choose a reward serving security, equipment, or opportunity.

Control the main character directly. Routine play combines movement/interactions and watching assigned tasks. An alien pet follows and learns useful behaviors through choices/training. Additional autonomous companions are optional encounters. Use illustrated 2D with C’s angled viewpoint as a reference; small detailed locations connected by travel are a tentative preference.

## Non-negotiable direction

- Meaningful spatial play; the concept must not collapse into illustrated text.
- Tactical/risk choices and visible security/access progress.
- Outside-only broadcast access; no stream-informed in-world rivals.
- A path beyond compulsory participation, rather than permanent entrapment.
- A visual process that works in motion and can produce more than one attractive scene.

## Practical boundary

Personal single-player on Mac first; 5–10 owner hours/week including decisions/testing. Flexible spending does not select particular purchases. No commercial or multiplayer commitment. Initial build tests one encounter, not the entire fiction. Engine, asset technique, final protagonist background and full campaign length remain open.
