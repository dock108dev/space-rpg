# Player guide

Start the game using the [quickstart](../README.md#quickstart).

Enter a name, choose an amber, teal or plum jacket, and Start new chapter. Appearance has no stat advantage. Blank names use Alex; long names keep the first 32 Unicode code points. Continue restores the last saved character. Earlier characters lists your other saved characters. New characters preserve those saves. Saves from other applications or prototype versions are not imported.

Type an intention and press Return, or use a contextual choice. Try `look around`, `inspect package`, `go to package`, or `move 2 up and 3 right`. Questions explain without acting. The interpreter understands supported actions and named targets, not arbitrary goals. Help explains controls; `look around` describes local targets. History retains full responses. The 100% / 125% switch enlarges all interface text and is also available on character setup. The preference is remembered; choices scroll when needed.

1. **Find shelter.** Try all three harmless powers, choose one, approach the package and enter the assessment. Defeat its creature, collect the package, then go to shelter and its desk. Choose orientation supplies. The pet follows; a traveler can join, wait or be declined.
2. **Prepare if you wish.** Go to the hub bench and claim the preparation kit: three base items and 20 carried material. Equip one item; improvements cost 4 each. Optional supplies yield one bundle, worth 4 only after explicit exchange. Access opens a shortcut; survey opens the expedition's dry latch. They pay no material. At the board, file settlement claim to earn your private home and 10 material once. Furnishings cost 2 each; storage costs 4. Stored material must be withdrawn explicitly.
3. **Choose your expedition.** The approach's north range is harmless practice; its containment breach is a separate repeatable danger. `enter expedition` at the east barrier begins the full relay assignment. No recruit, upgrade, home purchase or optional task is required.
4. **Clear the warden and cross.** A step, guard or shield costs 1 AP; a shot or blast costs 2. Range and clear sight matter. End turn lets eligible companions act, then the enemy responds and AP refreshes. It cancels the rest of your plan so you can choose again. If walking runs out of AP, End turn deliberately and repeat the destination command. Stop cancels future steps, while a committed animation finishes.
5. **Pick a route.** The live lane is short but hurts an unprotected protagonist and injures exposed companions. Shield, guard, weave and dash offer different preparation choices. For dry passage, use your recorded survey or go to the isolation wheel and turn it, then select maintenance. After the warden is cleared, `walk safely then go to drainage then go to core` removes AP refreshes on clear floor. Safe walking stops before live floor, obstruction, travel, pause or Stop; it never performs an enemy turn. Recover routing core beside the socket.
6. **Return and report.** Retreat gathers the whole joined party, preserving injuries and expedition progress. Go to the hub board and `file relay report`: one core delivered, 8 carried material paid exactly once. The chapter stays completed. Home, unfinished work, preparation and care remain usable.

Rest at shelter or home heals the protagonist for free. Injured pets cannot fetch; downed travelers cannot support. Shelter care costs 2 carried material per patient. With no funds, begin assisted pet/companion care and complete two attended rounds; this remains repeatable. Nobody dies permanently. The report records conditions at filing; later treatment changes current condition, not that history.

Click the world to leave typing and use WASD/arrows, E to interact, and Enter to end a turn. Tab navigates controls and Space activates buttons. Escape pauses. Use Pause → Save and quit. A failed save leaves the game open: retry, or use Restore save access for an interrupted writer. Continue never resumes old command queues. Safe walking must be enabled again after loading.

Supported minimum/native window: **1152×882 content**, logical canvas 1280×980. At 125%, interface font sizes increase from their individual baselines, rounded to whole sizes. Setup and Help reflow; Pause and its recovery details stay above the game. Silent presentation; no online model, provider, soundtrack or audience rewards. Home suppresses outside-audience presentation; characters never receive feed information.
