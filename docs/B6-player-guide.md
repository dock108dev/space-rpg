# No one left behind — B6 player guide

Double-click **Launch B6.command**. Requires installed Godot 4.6.2 Standard; supported native window is 1152 × 882. B6 saves separately in `dev-state/B6-practice-v1`; `B6_SAVE_DIR` overrides this. No owner-save migration, online service or model.

A1 is modern text-adventure command play; A1a is story and gameplay. Type these commands or choose the corresponding contextual buttons. Directions control your protagonist; companions act for themselves.

## Prepare and teach

Complete the opening assessment, collect the package, reach shelter and choose a reward. Invite the traveler, decline, or leave them waiting at shelter. All routes remain viable.

1. `go to hub then go to bench then claim preparation kit then equip guard weave`
2. `ask pet to fetch supplies` — wait for the actual return. The bundle and chewed-label consequence remain saved. Alternatively, an earlier successful pet cache fetch earns the same lesson eligibility.
3. `go to approach then go to range then test power then teach the pet cover fetch`
4. Improve equipment/power at the hub bench if desired. `go to shelter then go to desk then rest` restores your health freely.

Cover fetch costs nothing and is learned once after real retrieval. During each breach the healthy trained pet can bring one reachable cover plate before an enemy pulse, absorbing one pressure. The north range stays harmless. Training is useful but is not required to enter or survive danger.

## The containment breach

At the approach, `inspect breach` explains purpose, risk and escape. `go to breach then enter breach` enters actual danger beside the damaged latch. This is a bounded repeatable encounter, not the full expedition beyond the closed barrier. It awards no currency.

You have four action points per turn. `shoot creature` spends two; `use blast` spends two if chosen. `raise shield`, `guard`, a cardinal step or `dash left` spends one. Your B4 equipment and chosen-power improvements apply. Shots require range and line of sight; dash checks every square. Questions cost nothing.

`end turn` gives a joined healthy traveler one automatic shot at a visible target in range, then lets the trained pet retrieve reachable cover, then resolves one creature pulse. No turns pass on their own. `guard then end turn` is a useful way to watch support without spending all your points.

The first exposed pulse injures the pet, disabling fetch. The second can down the traveler, disabling their shot. They remain recoverable. Condition tint/rings and the downed pose accompany the narration. `How is the pet?` and `What can my companion do?` explain the actual state. Killing the creature prevents its next pulse; the encounter is completable with only your protagonist and pet.

`retreat` gathers the party into a rescue sling and follows the west route to the hub. Conditions remain. If floor access is blocked, `call evacuation` explicitly requests overhead extraction, forfeits the attempt and returns everyone without payment. Waiting/declined travelers remain at shelter. No one dies permanently or disappears.

## Care, including an empty wallet

`go to shelter then go to desk`. The **Companion care** choice opens a compact menu.

| Interaction | Result |
|---|---|
| `What does treatment cost?` | Reports terms and carried/stored balance; no spending |
| `Treat the pet` | 2 carried material; restores fetch and learned cover fetch |
| `Help the companion recover` | 2 carried material; restores autonomous support |
| `Begin assisted pet care` / `Begin assisted companion care` | Free alternative; two attended rounds, no offline recovery |
| `Continue assisted care` | Attend one six-second procedure; use twice to restore capability |
| `Cancel assistance` | Leaves injury intact and discards partial care progress |

With stored money, go home, go to locker, `withdraw 2 material`, then return to the desk. Care never silently spends storage. With no money anywhere, assistance remains usable repeatedly. Stay at the desk through its two rounds, or explicitly cancel before travel. Pause freezes the procedure; Stop cancels later queued work but lets a committed round finish. Save between rounds to resume later. Ordinary rest or entry into shelter/home never heals companions.

The kit supplies 20 once, optional bundle exchange 4 once, and settlement 10 once. All B4 improvements cost 16; three furnishings plus storage cost 10. Maximum purchases leave 4 total without optional exchange: enough for two paid treatments. Representative one-gear/one-power preparation plus all home purchases leaves 12. Free assistance handles repeated setbacks after those funds are gone. Rest remains free and rewards never replay.

## Continue and controls

`save and quit`, relaunch B6, then Continue. Conditions, trained behavior, encounter, recruitment, home/furniture/storage and resources restore together; old commands remain inactive. Failed writes roll the affected action back and retain earlier save material. Defeat uses the last living save, including its actual injuries and money.

`Stop` cancels future queued work without undoing completed damage or spending. Escape pauses, including focus loss; resume manually. Text focus protects movement keys; click the world or Tab out before direct controls. `Have the companion wait` works safely at shelter when healthy; `rejoin companion` returns them to the party there. Name an actor when treatment is ambiguous.

The interpreter supports bounded English and named targets, not arbitrary plans. Presentation remains silent, with rigid cutout motion, a hovering pet and mixed raster/vector scenery. Assisted care is a short attended procedure, not a medical simulation. B7 expedition, B8 polish, B9 standalone packaging and B10 owner beta acceptance remain separate. Engineering evidence does not establish owner acceptance.
