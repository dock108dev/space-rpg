# First experiment contract — EXP-001

Status: S04 integrated engineering gate passed September 9, 2026; exact Mac candidate and visual/runtime evidence in ../evidence/S04/README.md. S05 owner-play preparation complete; owner session/verdict unrun. The experience hypothesis and owner acceptance remain open.

## Question

Does a small illustrated spatial encounter make power preparation, action-point decisions, risk/reward, and a useful traveling pet appealing enough to continue—and can its visuals be reproduced?

## Proposed playable sequence

1. Begin in a small neighborhood-inspired location altered by the takeover.
2. See and select force blast, protective shield, or short dash.
3. Navigate to an orientation package; face deliberately released alien creatures.
4. Reach temporary shelter without an actual countdown.
5. Choose a persistent reward serving security, equipment, or opportunity.

The precise map, story order and transition are test assembly, not settled canon. One local encounter is sufficient. AP values are adjustable hypotheses. Creature intentions use visible behavior rather than explicit action labels. Audience reactions are outside-only flavor/feedback; no rival feed access.

## Pet and routine interaction

Include one alien pet following without blocking paths and one useful learned behavior with visible before/after difference. The precise behavior needs a small proposal in S04. Demonstrate direct walking and one assigned routine action. Do not add care meters, pet injury, a full training tree, or optional extra recruitment.

## Persistence

Support a defined manual/autosave policy and reload from the latest save in ordinary mode. Save chosen power, inventory/package, encounter/objective state, reward, and learned pet behavior at supported boundaries. Verify fresh state, reload and single-application rewards. Permanent-death mode comes later.

## Visual evidence

Selected C-inspired illustration in actual motion, including human and pet following, a creature cue, an effect, doorway readability, and second-asset repeatability. Technical and aesthetic verdicts remain separate. The concept board does not count as this evidence.

## Completion evidence

An identifiable locally runnable Mac artifact; documented setup; relevant functional checks; actual visual sample; reproducible second-asset recipe; owner observation and verdict. Review whether actions create meaningful choices and whether the owner wants to repeat. Keep unresolved defects and unrun checks explicit.

## Excluded from this experiment

Full campaign, freedom endings, employment, saved replay identities, audience economy, property/privacy implementation, additional companion recruitment, full pet progression, pet injury, permanent-death mode, multiplayer, offline progression and commercial packaging. These are deferred, not deleted from the intended game.

## Revision triggers

Tedious AP bookkeeping; unreadable creature behavior; pet obstruction; rewards that feel identical; inability to produce a second matching asset; art that disappoints in motion. Fix the failed dimension before adding content. Do not infer success from a launch or passing tests.
